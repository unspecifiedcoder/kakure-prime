Every Solana treasury reveals the strategy: positions, rebalances, recipients, and who approved them. Kah-koo-ray Prime changes that.

It starts with Kakure Wallet—a real browser extension, not a wallet page. The extension creates a Solana key locally, encrypts the vault, supports Devnet and Mainnet, and automatically locks. Websites receive a public address, never the private key.

The Connect Wallet action stays visible across the product. When Kakure needs a signature, the extension opens a dedicated review window showing the requesting site and the exact kind of request. Nothing is silently signed.

This is a real wallet path. A funded Kakure Wallet reviews the recipient and amount, signs a Devnet transfer inside the extension, and produces a finalized transaction that anyone can inspect in Solana Explorer.

Inside settlement, sponsor data does real work. The Pre Stocks lane accepts only official assets and checks their Token Twenty Twenty-Two mints. If a token moves outside its permitted mark, execution stops.

The Apple stock lane uses pith market data as a hard freshness and confidence gate. Missing or stale data means no settlement—not a guessed price.

For an eligible position, the fund creates a private note. An encrypted proposal collects three of five frost approvals. The prover verifies ownership, authorization, and policy before the state can move.

Authorized signers see the asset, amount, recipient, and approval state. The public chain sees a commitment, a spent nullifier, and proof—no ticker, no amount, no recipient, and no signer graph. A separate auditor quorum enables controlled disclosure when policy requires it.

The private rail is also connected to deployed Solana programs and a real Mee-tee-or-ah bonding-curve market.

Kah-koo-ray Prime gives tokenized equities private operations, threshold governance, and accountable settlement. Hide the strategy—not accountability.
